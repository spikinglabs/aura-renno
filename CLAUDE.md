# Aura

Serverless baby and child log (PWA). Everything is in `src/app.html`; `sh build.sh` wraps it into `app/index.html` (not committed).

- No own server. Data is in IndexedDB (`aura` database, store `kv`, key `state`; older localStorage `aura.v2` is moved over on load) and optionally one JSON file in the user's Google Drive (`drive.file` scope), merged per item by `updatedAt` with `deleted: true` tombstones. Add any new synced collection to `SYNCED` (now: children, episodes, entries, meds, measurements, growth, products, vaccines, checkups, dental, appts, emergency, docs, pages).
- Log format version is `SCHEMA` in `src/app.html`. A new collection or new fields need no version change: older apps keep parts they don't know. Changing what existing data means needs `SCHEMA + 1`, a `MIGRATIONS` step and a test that upgrades a saved old log. The log as it was is kept as `before-vN` (phone) and `backups/aura-log-before-vN.json` (Drive), and an older app never writes a newer log.
- The app never gives medical, dose or diagnosis advice; it only shows reminders and when a medicine's minimum gap has passed. AI prompts must keep that rule, and every AI call goes through `ai()`, which swaps names for placeholders and talks to Anthropic or any OpenAI-style chat API the user configures; photos, sounds, links and birth dates never go into a prompt. The one exception is reading document pages: with "Read new and replaced pages with AI" on, a page of the vaccination book or U-Heft that is added or replaced goes to the parent's own AI service (key needed, asked once, the sheet says names on the page can't be hidden), and the answer is only a review list; nothing is saved until checked.
- Partners share by Google Drive permissions on the one log file (joiner picks it with the Google Picker). Sound clips (max 30 s) are stored inline in entries like photos and document pages (data: URLs), so they sync to partners and go into every backup. Drive backups keep each data: URL once, as a file in `backups/media` named by its SHA-256, and point to it (`aura-media:<file>#<header>`); restoring puts it back in (`packMedia`/`unpackMedia`). A log over 5 MB is written with a resumable upload (`writeFile`).
- Product recalls come from L's Recall Tracker (spikinglabs/recall-tracker): one public JSON file on R2 (`RECALL_URL`), fetched by the phone at most every 12 h and cached locally in `S.recalls` (not synced). The family's product list (`products`) is matched on the phone and never sent anywhere.
- Documents are digital twins of paper documents (`docs` + `pages`, one item per page, in the paper's order by `order`): the parent keeps the pages up to date (add, replace in place, move, delete) and the digital entries follow from them. The vaccination book (`vax-<child>`) and U-Heft (`u-<child>`) are document types with a reader in `DOC_TYPES`; any other document is pages and a note. A new document type with entries gets a `read` (prompt + schema) and a review step. Until v3 the book photos lived in `vaccines`/`checkups` as `kind: 'page'` (moved by `MIGRATIONS[2]`).
- Notes (including doctor's notes) are free text, not forms.
- Daily tracking is customizable per child (`TRACKERS`, `child.track`, defaults from `suggestedTrack` by age). Anything tied to a tracker (quick button, summary card, timeline rows, doctor table column) must hide when it is off.
- Design is "Night light": warm dark palette at night, warm cream by day, tokens on `:root` and `:root[data-mode="day"]`. Must work at 375 px width without sideways scrolling.
- Run `npm test` before pushing (set `CHROMIUM_PATH` if Playwright's own browser is not installed). Pushes to `main` deploy to GitHub Pages.
- Vaccinations (`vaccines`: doses as written in the book; its pages are a document) are a screen under the Documents tab. The schedule (`VAX_PLAN`, German STIKO as of 2026) only reminds of the usual ages; the pediatrician decides. Update it when STIKO changes.
- The Documents tab (`renderDocs`) lists the emergency card, Appointments, Vaccinations, Checkups, Dental checkups and other documents; those four are screens under it (`tabOf` lights the Documents tab).
- Emergency card (`emergency`: one record per child with allergies, conditions, regular medicines, blood group, pediatrician, insurance, contacts) opens from the card button next to the child at the top of every main screen and the sick view, from the child's sheet, and from the home-screen shortcut (`?open=card`, manifest `shortcuts`), uses the doctor-summary language (`dt()`), and its allergies, conditions and medicines also go into the doctor summary and AI handover. Contacts and insurance never go into a prompt.
- Growth percentiles use the WHO tables embedded as `WHO` (L, M, S per month: 2006 standards to 5 years, 2007 reference after; weight to 10 y, height to 19 y, head to 5 y). They need the child's `sex` (`f`/`m`) and birth date, and only describe; no wording that judges a percentile.
- Checkups (`checkups`: German U1–U10/J1 visits from the yellow U-Heft; its pages are a document) are a screen under the Documents tab. `U_PLAN` holds the usual time and the period a checkup can be done (G-BA Kinder-Richtlinie; U10 added August 2026, paid from 2027); update it when the rules change. Due checkups and vaccinations can go into the parent's own calendar as a Google Calendar link or an .ics file; Aura never signs in to a calendar.
- Dental checkups (`dental`: German Z1–Z6 at the dentist's, 6 to 72 months; their pages are the dental pass document `z-<child>`, whose reader also maps old FU1a/b/c and FU2 names) are a screen under the Documents tab. `Z_PLAN` holds the periods (G-BA zahnärztliche Früherkennung, as of 2026; since 2026 they can also be written in the U-Heft, so the U-Heft reader routes Z entries to `dental`). A visit can add teeth the dentist saw to the tooth map (`growth`), and the Growth teeth card links to the dental checkups.
- Booked appointments (`appts`: one per checkup or dental checkup, and one vaccination appointment can cover several vaccines (`ks`, `doses`), with date, optional time and a note) show instead of the usual time on the schedule row, the Today line and the Documents tab until the visit is recorded; once the day has passed Aura asks to record it, and the recorded visit starts on that day. Add to calendar uses the appointment's date and time. The Appointments screen (`renderAppts`, under Documents) lists them all, plus any other appointment (`plan: 'other'`, `title`, optional linked `docId`; past ones move to Past, nothing to record). Saving a coming appointment offers the calendar right away (Google link, .ics, or sharing the .ics); entries carry time, place (`place`), note and a link back (`?open=appts`).
- Kita (daycare) papers are ordinary `docs` (`type: 'other'`) with `kita` set to a `KITA_KINDS` key (contract, measles, note, other); the Documents tab lists them in their own card with the measles doses already recorded (facts only, the Kita decides what it accepts). kidsfox, the family's Kita app, has no API or export, so nothing connects to it.
