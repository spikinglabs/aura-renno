Allo Auri privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Auri is a baby and child log that runs in your browser. It has no server of its own and no accounts. We, the makers of Auri, never receive, see or store your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you log (children, feeds, sleep, temperatures, medicines, notes, photos and sound recordings) is stored in your browser on your device. Deleting the app's data or using "Delete everything on this phone" removes it.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Auri asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With it, Auri can create and update its own files in your Drive (a folder named "Aura", Auri's earlier name with your log, its backups and a PDF copy of each document you keep in Auri) and open files you pick yourself. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. The data goes directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any Auri server.

If you share your log with a partner from inside Auri, Google gives that person's Google account access to the one log file. You can remove their access in Auri or in Google Drive at any time.

Auri's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Auri does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to anyone else.

AI helpers (optional)

AI features are off until you add a key for an AI service you choose. When you tap an AI button, the text needed for that task is sent directly from your device to that service, with names replaced by placeholders. Photos, sound recordings, links and birth dates are never sent, with one exception you choose: when you turn on reading pages of the vaccination book or U-Heft, a page you add or replace goes to your AI service so it can read the entries. Names printed on the page can't be hidden in a photo. You check what it read before anything is saved. That service's own privacy terms apply to what it receives.

Contact

Questions: write to the developer contact email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Auri.
